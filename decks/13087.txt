Deck Name: Ladies of Power
Description:
This is my take on Aaradhya and the Path of Power path, specifically for big tournaments or tournaments where you can expect more bleed and little combat decks.
I tried to slim the deck down, make it more consistent, less explosive, so as not to put a target on my back in every game.
