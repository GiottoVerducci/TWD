Deck Name: Caw caw grinder
Description:
I was inspired some years ago by Jonas Ståhle's antiventrue grinder with animalism in which Carrion crows had great synergy with Hidden strength. I was very excited when we got Djeneba and Barachiel with animalism, even though I needed to add a bit of off-clan crypt support and discipline masters. Djeneba's stealth is amazing for both Govern the unaligned superior which rarely gets blocked and a sneaky Deep song to hit the bouncer off the table.
