Deck Name: Beauty and the Beast
Created by: Otso Saariluoma
Description:
This deck was created by Otso and brought into the TWD by Tommi Hakomaa. Minor changes were made compared to Otso's version. I added 2x Carrion Crows for 2x Pursuits, since I could not find 2 more pursuits while building the deck. Also 1x Yawp Court that I did not draw a single time.
It's a very well built deck, that is hard to navigate but very fun to play.
Could maybe use 1x Dragonbound/1x Augury of Doomor/1x Tension in the Ranks to help ousting.
Pillar's Fall might also work.
In the prelim rounds, i got destroyed by FOR prevention, so Soulgrinder could also find its way in here.
Cross Table rescues must be punished.
