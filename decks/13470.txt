Deck Name: Banu Hakim Primogens

Gameplan -
Kalida is obv the best one, but Khadija was really handy with 3 superior so I might add one instead of greg or haave one less Samira. Samira was a good combo with Adisa though to get up 2 for 8.

My plan was to go pretty slow in the start and bleed a little to get the edge and prepare to finish my prey. I got 5-6 vampires in round 1-3 but had to make due with Khadija, Arjun and Adisa in the finals after Samira was stolen by my predators Chameleon... I made due thanks to Arthole and Khadija. One early played and one later Web of knives was a really good addition to my vampires most games.

Don't go too hard in the beginning so the table doesn't go against you. Also be mindful of the blood on your vampires, villein is obv great but don't waste it by hitting things for one each to save cards.

Tournament:
I was worried before the tournament that I would stack masters but instead I had a hard timing finding Haqim's law. Heart is really important and I was thinkling beforehand if I should add some Magic and some other eq. I didn't find room for respected representative or archon before the tournament and luckily enough I didn't miss them playwise.

I was suprised how good the stealth was and I almost flet like I was playing my usual toolbox decks with all the small decisions every round on how much and in what order I should bleed, hunt, resque etc. The ousting power with many small bleeds with some stealth each time was suprisingly strong.

The combat cards was mainly for surviving combat and adding bleedpower. But luckily my combat power was highly overesimated by most players during the tournament. I didn't think I would get to use Camarillas iron fist but it gave me alot of VPs and forward momentum.

Shoutout to Randy for great advise on both deck and playstyle.
