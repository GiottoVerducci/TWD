Deck Name: 11 nightmares

Inspired by Kalle Blomgrens 'I have a dream', I removed all the normal masters and added 10 Dreams and 1 House of Sorrow (pseudo-Dream) to my otherwise fairly standard Ravnos deck.

Bringing out new vampires every turn is key, and bringing out your smallest vampire on turn 1 to start putting down blood to uncontrolled with Break the Bonds is probably the best start you can get.
