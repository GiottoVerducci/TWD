Deck Name: Panyum panyum
Description:
This deck was designed while I was in a very long trip in South America. While travelling with my bycicle I stopped in Santiago de Chile, where I printed the whole deck and played it with the local group.
Gonzalo liked the deck and gave me a copy of Asguresh, which I used in this tournament.

A lot of decisions in this deck were around how to win against mono-ANI decks, which were popular at the time.

Small fun trivia; Asguresh torporised an Enkidu in this tournament in one round, full blood, with scattershoot and blur.
