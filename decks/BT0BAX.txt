Deck Name: Nephandi Motolove Coqueluche

[2026-11-18]
Tweaks and optimizations by Rafael Rezende.
Inspiration list by Tiago Honorato Ramos.

Comments: Tiago's list shares a similar foundation with Laurent Ribeyre's 2023 list, with his leaning more toward fast bleeds using Beatrice for heavy 3-bleeds and Conditioning for surprise ousts.

My list focuses more on establishing an impactful board presence from the mid- to late-game, running a different Master package with less early explosiveness alongside additional reactions, giving it solid resilience once the game develops.

The secret to the deck lies in understanding that discarding must be done deliberately, requiring constant table reading—especially when using the very few copies of Delaying Tactics (only 2).

The deck performs very well without Shard and The Parthenon; it obviously loses a bit of its explosive potential, yet remains completely playable.

It is no secret for the Nephandi archetype that the key card is undoubtedly The Unmasking, which is included in slightly higher numbers (3) compared to standard Molotov lists (usually only 2).
